# Interview Questions Template

## North Star Progress
- Quantifiable progress on this week

## Last Week Review
- Last Week's Objective (one sentence)
- Completion Percentage (0-100)
- Last Week's Progress (2-4 bullet points describing outcomes)

## This Week Planning
- This Week's Objective (one sentence, clear and specific)
- Concrete Steps for This Week (3-5 actionable steps)

## Reflections & Notes
- Big Wins (1-5 significant accomplishments or breakthroughs)
- Blockers and Asks (challenges you're facing and support you need)
- Upcoming Opportunities (events, connections, or possibilities on the horizon)
- Additional Notes (any other context or reflections)
